ADR-017.md

Two CS-1 seed-data gaps are recorded here rather than filled with invented
values:

1. `PurchaseOrder.po_number` is nullable, and `po_4488`'s row is seeded
   with `po_number = None`. `05_Downstream_Reference_Execution_Trace.md`
   states this PO's SAP-style number as "(not stated in trace)"; no other
   frozen or reference document supplies one. Inventing a plausible-looking
   10-digit number would misrepresent seeded, traceable data as if it were
   a literal frozen figure.

2. `req_5203` (Scenario B's third canonical artifact — the branch-conduit
   item, `Canonical_Demo_Dataset.md` §10, "Delivery/requisition (branch
   conduit)... not yet a PO... vendor: not yet committed") is **not**
   seeded in CS-1. It is structurally a `PurchaseRequisition` — an entity
   the approved plan scopes to CS-2, not CS-1 — and `Canonical_Demo_Dataset.md`
   itself states it has no vendor. `PurchaseOrder.vendor_id` is a required
   field; representing `req_5203` as a `PurchaseOrder` with a fabricated
   vendor would invent a commercial relationship the frozen documents do
   not state, which the user's authorization explicitly forbids ("Do not
   invent commercial relationships that are not grounded in the frozen
   documents"). CS-1 therefore seeds `po_5201` and `po_5202` (Scenario B's
   two real, fully-specified POs) and defers `req_5203` to CS-2, when
   `PurchaseRequisition` exists to hold it correctly, per its own actual
   entity type.

Reason:

Both gaps were identified during CS-1 implementation, not anticipated in
the approved plan's §13 seed-data strategy, which listed `req_5203` under
CS-1's seed target without noting that its own entity type is CS-2 scope.
Recorded here per the explicit implementation-authorization instruction to
"record any contradiction you find instead of silently resolving it" —
this is not a contradiction between frozen documents, but a scope-boundary
gap in the plan's own seed-data list, surfaced rather than quietly patched
over by inventing data.

Consequences:

- CS-1's canonical seed assertion test checks for exactly `po_4471`,
  `po_4488`, `po_4512` (Scenario A) and `po_5201`, `po_5202` (Scenario B) —
  five `PurchaseOrder` rows, not six.
- `req_5203` remains specification-only (`Canonical_Demo_Dataset.md` §10)
  until CS-2 implements `PurchaseRequisition`, at which point it must be
  seeded through that entity's own real domain state machine, matching
  the discipline already proven for every other seeded row in this
  system and in the Reference Engineering System.
