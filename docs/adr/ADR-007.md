ADR-007.md

RES-4 introduces DesignChange as a single Reference Engineering entity for
engineering-authorization instruments (ASI / CCD / Bulletin), with a closed
type enum and an engineering-document lifecycle
(DRAFT -> ISSUED -> ACKNOWLEDGED, SUPERSEDED / VOID as terminal states).

Reason:

**Engineering vs Commercial ownership boundary.** The Reference Engineering
System owns DesignChange as an *engineering authorization record* only. The
authoritative `The Reference Engineering System.md` §16 lists ChangeOrder as a
DesignChange subtype and ChangeEvent/PCO/COR as RES entities, but RES-4's
approved boundary relocates pricing and settlement to the future Reference
Commercial System. RES-4 therefore implements ASI, CCD and Bulletin but NOT
ChangeOrder, ChangeEvent, PCO or COR — pricing, contract-sum adjustment and
settlement are out of Engineering's scope. This deliberately diverges from the
frozen synthesized list; that divergence is the point of this ADR.

**Type strategy.** ASI, CCD and Bulletin are one entity distinguished by a
closed type enum (`type` in {ASI, CCD, BULLETIN}), not three entity classes.
They share identical fields and lifecycle; only the authority instrument
label differs. A single entity avoids duplicated code and keeps the state
machine one. ChangeOrder is deliberately absent from the enum (Commercial).

**Lifecycle choice.** Engineering authorization is tracked as
DRAFT -> ISSUED -> ACKNOWLEDGED, with a non-issued/non-forward SUPERSEDED (a
later DesignChange replaced this one) and a VOID only reachable from DRAFT or
ISSUED. This is intentionally NOT the earlier-drafted
PROPOSED -> AUTHORIZED -> INCORPORATED lifecycle: PROPOSED/AUTHORIZED/
INCORPORATED blur the boundary into pricing and drawing incorporation, both of
which belong elsewhere.

**Why drawing incorporation is a relationship, not a state.** A DesignChange
becomes effective in the drawings when an affected DrawingVersion is issued /
superseded. That is expressed as a relationship
(`affected_drawing_version_ids` plus DrawingVersion supersession via
IssueDrawingVersion), not by a DesignChange "INCORPORATED" state. Keeping
incorporation out of the DesignChange lifecycle lets a change remain
authorized while its drawing follow-up is still in flight, and avoids modeling
a cross-entity effect as an entity-local state.

**Why a direct DesignChange <-> Submittal relationship is deferred.** A design
change can invalidate a submittal, but that effect is derivable through shared
affected drawings/specs rather than a first-class link. No direct
DesignChange-Submittal relationship is introduced in RES-4.

**Why FieldIssue and Commercial change artifacts are deferred.** Field Issue /
Observation, and the Commercial ChangeEvent/PCO/COR/ChangeOrder chain, are
outside RES-4's engineering-authorization scope (FieldIssue is RES-5;
Commercial artifacts belong to the Reference Commercial System).

Implementation: a single `DesignChange` dataclass with `DESIGN_CHANGE_TYPES`
and `DESIGN_CHANGE_STATUSES` closed string vocabularies (same convention as
RFI/DrawingVersion), a pure `design_change_transitions` state machine, and
domain-invariant validation of type/status in the entity. No ORM, migration,
repository, API, use case, webhook, seed, or frontend change ships with
RES-4A.
